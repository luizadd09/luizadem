A low poly diorama of a small countryside farm, made for a DAE assignment.

The mood was inspired by the film *The VVitch* and by Ukrainian villages: a painted red gate, a brick barn with a black goat on its roof, a house with blue folk ornaments, a crooked fence and bare trees under a grey sky — the whole scene kept to around 21,000 triangles.

Rotate and zoom the model in the 3D viewer, or [open it on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dae-diorama-retake-small-farm-fd2ac09310c94e9e8dce881e6f304afb).
