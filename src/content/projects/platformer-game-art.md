A gothic graveyard world for a 2D platformer — moody and a little eerie, but playful at heart.

**Art direction.** Twilight purples, blues and muted reds, a crescent moon over the graves, and a light grain texture on every object. Low contrast keeps the mood unsettling, while a complementary palette makes the heroine stand out.

**Characters.** A small plague-doctor reaper in a frilly dress, and a multi-eyed angel whose stare grabs your attention the moment it appears.

**World.** Rolling hills of tombstones, crosses, dead trees and a crooked church, layered from a dark foreground to a misty background for depth.

**The challenge** was keeping it gothic without becoming grim. Soft shapes, cute animations and small whimsical touches — like a tea party with a ghost on the start screen — keep the game fun to play.

Backgrounds and characters were painted in Photoshop, then brought into the game in Unity.
