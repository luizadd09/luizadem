I wanted to make a personal illustration — something that could work as my LinkedIn banner and as a visual for my portfolio website, and that feels like me rather than a generic header.

The idea came from Ukrainian folklore. In old myths, a witch could turn into a cat with a woman's face and wander the village at night unnoticed. I gathered old engravings of witches, brooms and half-human creatures as inspiration, then sketched the cat-woman in different poses until I found the right mix of elegance and mischief: a long leaping body, a curling tail, a white headband with a flower and a beaded necklace borrowed from traditional Ukrainian dress.
